The label's "fourth full-length" designation is a marketing number that includes the earlier full-length/compilation convention. This guide follows the six-studio-album classification, so it counts this as the third studio album, after _Kiss Land_ and _Beauty Behind the Madness_.

Live, this is a stretch worth enjoying for the big sing-alongs and the contrast of light.
