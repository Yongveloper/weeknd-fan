According to official information, parking inside the stadium is not available for this concert. The parking experiences and alternatives from other concerts below do not automatically apply to this one.

- After the show, entry to Daehwa Station · 대화역 was controlled from the entrance, taking over 30 minutes when crowded, and taxis were nearly impossible to catch. Walking to Kintex Station · 킨텍스역 or Juyeop Station · 주엽역 is frequently mentioned as an alternative. — [Community review, 2026-08](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=bigbangvip&no=270100)
- Kintex Station · 킨텍스역 (GTX-A) is about 1.8km away, a 20-minute walk (per Interpark), and 17 minutes to Seoul Station · 서울역. During Coldplay (2025-04), a shuttle from Kintex Station Exit 2 · 킨텍스역 2번 출구 ran from 22:00 with 5 buses at 2–3 minute intervals for 300 won. — [OhmyNews, 2025-04](https://www.ohmynews.com/NWS_Web/View/at_pg.aspx?CNTN_CD=A0003119116)
- There is a reported case of a temporary shuttle (route 98-1) operating between Kintex Station · 킨텍스역 and the stadium during a concert. — [Namuwiki](https://namu.wiki/w/고양종합운동장%20주경기장)
- Getting cars out of parking took 1–2 hours, and the stadium parking lot was already full by morning. Temporary parking at Kintex · 킨텍스 and the Ilsanseo-gu Office Parking Tower · 일산서구청 주차타워 (6,000 won per day) are mentioned as alternatives. — [Concert review, 2026-04](https://moneyroan.com/bts-goyang-concert-traffic-parking-2026/), [Community post, 2025-04](https://www.teamblind.com/kr/post/고양-종합운동장-콘서트-후-귀가-방법-추천-부탁-LwinPVPz)
