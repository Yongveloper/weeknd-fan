The entire floor is divided into two standing sections, A and B, with a cross-shaped runway extending from the stage to the center of the floor, ending in a small B-stage. The front-row strip of the standing area is the Early Entry Package section.

The stands form rings across floors 1–3, with section codes made up of an English letter followed by a number. The upper corners to the left and right of the stage are limited-view seats. Each section's floor, location, and price tier follow the original seating chart and the ticketing page.
