The entire floor is divided into two standing sections, A and B, with a cross-shaped runway extending from the stage to the center of the floor, ending in a small B-stage. The front-row strip of the standing area is the Early Entry Package section.

The stands form rings across floors 1–3, with section codes made up of an English letter followed by a number. The upper corners to the left and right of the stage are limited-view seats. Each section's floor, location, and grade follow the original seating chart and the ticketing page.

### Seating and entry terms on the official detail page

These come from the ticketing platform's official detail page (English). — [NOL World show page](https://world.nol.com/en/ticket/places/26000511/products/26006903)

- Viewing positions inside standing sections A and B are not assigned. Standing ticket holders enter in the order of the admission numbers given at booking.
- If you arrive after standing admission has begun and miss your admission number, there is no refund. Delays caused by traffic, parking or other personal reasons are not refunded either.
- The Early Entry Package includes one standing ticket, early entry to the standing area, exclusive merchandise, an exclusive laminate, lanyard and wristband, and priority access to merchandise purchases. The early-entry time and procedure have not been announced yet.
- Some seats in sections T1 and T2 on the 1st floor are table seats. In every section except E3, E4, E9, E10, W5 and W6, seats booked together may still have a gap between them because of the venue layout.
- Exact wheelchair seats within the wheelchair sections on the seating chart are assigned on the day. Only the purchaser can collect the tickets, at the on-site ticket box on the day (no proxy collection or transfer), and must show both a disability registration card (or welfare card) and a physical government-issued ID. Without them, neither the purchaser nor the companion can enter.
- In the VIP packages, the Ultimate VIP (859,000 won) is a P seat in T1 on the 1st floor and the Gold VIP (569,000 won) a P seat in E4 on the 1st floor. The Ultimate includes a group photo opportunity on stage before the show, and the official page states that no VIP package includes artist participation. — [NOL World VIP package page](https://world.nol.com/en/ticket/places/26000537/products/26007178)
