- At the Coldplay concert (2025-04), the standing-area waiting spot was the **auxiliary stadium**, and reviews recommended arriving 3 hours before the show. — [Concert review, 2025-04](https://vitariaplus.com/entry/콜드플레이-내한-후기-2025-고양종합운동장-스탠딩-콜드플레이-내한-공연-첫째-날-리얼-후기)
- At the same concert, plastic bottles of 500mL or larger were not allowed in, and 500mL cartons of water were sold on-site for 1,000 won (not given out free). — same review
- An Interpark notice for a different Goyang concert stated that standing-area entry followed queue-number order, and numbers became invalid after the entry start time. — [Interpark notice for another concert](http://mticket.interpark.com/Goods/GoodsInfo/GoodsExpandPopup?GoodsCode=08004615&ViewMode=GoodsDetailHtml)
- One review describes buying drinks in advance at the CU convenience store across from the stadium and removing the bottle cap before entering. — [Community review, 2026-08](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=bigbangvip&no=270100)
