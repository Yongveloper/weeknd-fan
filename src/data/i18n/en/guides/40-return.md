### Last Trains and Routes Home

- According to an unofficial timetable, the last weekday Line 3 train from Daehwa Station · 대화역 departs sometime between midnight and 12:30 AM ([unofficial timetable](http://subway.koreatriptips.com/subway-station/SES03/SUB310.html)). Since the concert may end later than scheduled, confirm the exact time on the day using station announcements and the [Korail metropolitan subway map](https://dev2.letskorail.com/images/subway_map.pdf).
- When Daehwa Station · 대화역 is under crowd control, Kintex Station · 킨텍스역 (GTX-A, a 20-minute walk — per Interpark) is an alternative.
- A **paid shuttle bus** running between Kintex Station Exit 1 · 킨텍스역 1번 출구 and Goyang Stadium · 고양종합운동장 is also an option for getting home. The operating hours, fare, interval, and stadium boarding location have not yet been announced.
- For the paid Kakao T shuttle, **the return-trip time and boarding location may change depending on conditions on the day**, and a notification text will be sent if they change. Check for the notification text on the day of the concert.
- Check real-time bus arrivals at [Goyang City BIS](https://bis.goyang.go.kr/m).

Congestion and parking experiences based on reviews are collected under the **Getting Home** tab in On-Site Tips.
