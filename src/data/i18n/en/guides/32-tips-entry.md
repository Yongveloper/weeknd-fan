- **You have to clear an adult-verification check on the day to get in.** Anyone under 19 is turned away even with a ticket: the 7 October show admits people born on or before 7 October 2007, and the 8 October show those born on or before 8 October 2007. — [NOL show notices](https://nol.yanolja.com/ticket/products/26006903#important-info)
- **Official guidance:** bring ID — for Korean nationals a resident registration card, driver's licence, passport or mobile ID; for foreign nationals a passport or alien registration card. After passing the check at an adult-check booth you must collect an adult-check wristband. — [Hyundai Card official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd5iIpMiQKE/)
- **Official notice:** lost or stolen tickets are not reissued under any circumstances, even with photo ID and the booking confirmation email. — [NOL World show page](https://world.nol.com/en/ticket/places/26000511/products/26006903)
- Cross one crosswalk from Exit 3 of Daehwa Station · 대화역 and you are at the stadium (about a 3-minute walk). — [OhmyNews, 2025-04](https://www.ohmynews.com/NWS_Web/View/at_pg.aspx?CNTN_CD=A0003119116)
- The notice for the Lim Young-woong Goyang shows (2026-09) listed umbrellas, large cameras, selfie sticks, outside food and drink, banners and laser pointers as prohibited, said a screenshot of a ticket QR is not accepted, and required ID. It said the merchandise booth is set up outside, before entry. — [Concert review, 2026-07](https://www.tndlrs.com/2026/07/herolanding3.html)
- Lockers at Daehwa Station · 대화역 ran well short on show days; one review describes using a reservation-based storage service near Exit 4. — [Community review, 2026-08](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=bigbangvip&no=270100)
- **Official venue map:** lockers are marked around the stadium near gate W1 and in the merchandise area. Hours, fees and numbers have not been announced. — [Hyundai Card official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd8N44RiTng/)
- At the BTS shows (2026-04) many police and stewards were posted along the entry and exit routes, and the venue emptied section by section. — [Community review, 2026-04](https://www.clien.net/service/board/park/19176744)
