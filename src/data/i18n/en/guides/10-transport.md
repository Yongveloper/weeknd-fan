Coming out of **Daehwa Station (Line 3) Exit 3 · 대화역(3호선) 3번 출구**, you cross one crosswalk to reach the stadium. During the Coldplay concert in April 2025, partial construction at the station reduced the number of usable exits ([OhmyNews, 2025-04](https://www.ohmynews.com/NWS_Web/View/at_pg.aspx?CNTN_CD=A0003119116)).

From **GTX-A Kintex Station Exit 1 · GTX-A 킨텍스역 1번 출구**, it's about a 20-minute walk. Any separate paid transport for this concert should be confirmed via the official transport information above.

**Buses** on the routes in the table below stop at the Daehwa Station stop · 대화역 정류장 and the Goyang Stadium/Ilsanseo-gu Office stop · 고양종합운동장·일산서구청 정류장. Check real-time arrivals at [Goyang City BIS](https://bis.goyang.go.kr/m).
