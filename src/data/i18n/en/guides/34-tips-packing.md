- Restroom lines ran 20–30 minutes, and many reviews say it's better to use the restroom at Daehwa Station · 대화역 or a nearby building before entering. Restaurants near Daehwa Station · 대화역 get crowded, so the area behind Juyeop Station · 주엽역 and Ilsan Paik Hospital · 일산백병원 is mentioned as an alternative. — [Community review, 2026-08](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=bigbangvip&no=270100)
- On the day of a large concert, every business in the commercial area around Daehwa Station · 대화역 had a line, and the station restrooms were extremely crowded too. — [OhmyNews, 2025-04](https://www.ohmynews.com/NWS_Web/View/at_pg.aspx?CNTN_CD=A0003119116)
- The west side of the stadium is open, letting the northwest wind blow straight in, and complaints of cold are common even at spring and autumn night concerts. — [Namuwiki](https://namu.wiki/w/고양종합운동장%20주경기장)
- One review recommends bringing outerwear, a blanket, and a portable battery, plus opera glasses if you're in the back rows of the 1st floor or on the 2nd floor or higher. Umbrellas have been banned at some events, making disposable raincoats an alternative. — [Concert guide](https://lingoculture.kr/goyang-stadium-concert-seating-guide/)
