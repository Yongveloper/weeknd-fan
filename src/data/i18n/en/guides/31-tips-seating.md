- The center of the 1st floor is close enough to see facial expressions, and the center of the W section (main stand) on the 2nd floor is often cited as a good spot for viewing the overall production. Many reviews say the 3rd floor feels too far away for full immersion. — [Section-by-section sightline reviews, updated 2026-05](https://alljoylog.com/고양종합운동장-좌석별-시야/)
- In the upper N and W sections, the railing reportedly blocks the view; the upper S section is said to be the farthest from the stage, and side seats reportedly have some sound loss. — same review
- A review of watching BTS (2026-04) from the 3rd floor reports that the view wasn't bad, thanks to the large screens. — [Community review, 2026-04](https://www.clien.net/service/board/park/19176744)
- At the BLACKPINK concert (2025-07), the organizer issued refunds after complaints about poor sightlines in some sections. The sections marked as limited-view seats are in the upper left and right corners of the original seating chart. — [Daum News, 2025-08](https://v.daum.net/v/20250816174638726)
