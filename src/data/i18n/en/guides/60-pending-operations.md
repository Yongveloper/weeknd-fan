NOL has stated that detailed entry guidance for the show day will be posted separately on the ticketing page one week before the concert.

Rules and operational practices from other concerts are not carried over as confirmed information for this concert. The existence of the shuttle bus service and its route have been confirmed via an official notification text, and the operating hours, fare, interval, and stadium boarding location will be updated once the organizer or ticketing platform issues an announcement.

The show-day schedule, adult check, standing entry rules and venue map were confirmed by Hyundai Card's official announcement and moved to "Show-Day Timetable". The venue map shows gate numbers but not which gate each seating section uses, and for Early Entry the only announcement so far is that buyers take the adult check and collect a wristband and gift at the dedicated booth; the priority admission time and method have not been announced yet.

Booking terms for standing, Early Entry and wheelchair seating (entry by admission number, what the package includes, how wheelchair tickets are collected) are in the Seating Guide.

Experiences reported repeatedly in reviews of other concerts are collected separately under On-Site Tips.
